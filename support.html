Frequently asked

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and — if you use iCloud — in your own private iCloud database. DramHub has no server with your data and no user accounts. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device.

The label scan did not read my bottle correctly.

Hold the bottle so the whole label is in frame, in good light, and turn it slowly — DramHub keeps reading while you move. Values read from the label can be edited any time under “Edit details” on the note or bottle. If a bottle is consistently misread, a photo of the label by email helps me improve recognition.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → DramHub. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period.

Is there an Android version?

No. DramHub is built for iPhone, and Android is not planned for launch.

I found a mistake in a distillery portrait.

Thank you — corrections are welcome at any time. Email the portrait's name and the correction, ideally with a source, and I will check and update it.
