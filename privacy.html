Hosting and server logs

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and embeds no third-party content — no Google fonts, no maps, no platform videos, no social buttons. Fonts, images and videos are served from the same server as the page. Your language choice (German/English) is stored locally in your browser only and never transmitted.

The legal basis is the initiation or performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or my legitimate interest in responding (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Mail is handled by Apple (iCloud Mail). I delete messages once your request is settled and no retention duties apply. A TestFlight invitation is issued through Apple's TestFlight service, for which I pass your email address to Apple — I will tell you before I do.

3 · The DramHub app

DramHub is built so that everything the app knows about you stays with you. There is no user account, no sign-up and no DramHub server holding personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Your collection, tasting notes, bottle photos, Lab productions, learning progress and Dram Signature are stored exclusively on your iPhone. If you use Apple's iCloud sync, this data is kept in your private iCloud database, accessible only to you with your Apple ID — not to DramHub. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

To capture bottles, DramHub uses your device's camera. Label recognition runs entirely on the device (Apple Vision); no images are sent to DramHub or anyone else. Photos are taken only with your consent and stored locally.

Location (optional)

Two features can use your location, each only after your explicit permission in iOS: naming a tasting location (address and nearby places via Apple Maps) and weather-based occasions (Apple WeatherKit). Your location is transmitted to Apple for this purpose and is not stored by DramHub. You can switch both off at any time in the app's settings or in iOS Settings. Weather data: Apple Weather, data sources.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are handled exclusively by Apple's App Store. DramHub receives no payment details and no personal information; the app only learns whether a subscription is active.

Retail links (advertising)

For some bottles DramHub shows links to retailers, marked as advertising. Tapping one leaves the app; the retailer is responsible for data processing on its site. DramHub transmits no personal data in doing so.

Usage statistics

At launch DramHub collects no usage statistics. Should anonymous, data-minimal statistics be introduced later, this will happen only with your explicit opt-in and will be described here and in the app.

Contributing (optional, planned)

A planned feature lets you contribute anonymous facts about bottles — cask type or strength, say — to improve the catalogue. This is voluntary and never includes anything about you or your collection.

4 · Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Because app data lives on your device, you can inspect, export and delete it there yourself; deleting the app removes all local data. For any request, email david@dramhub.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
